🪐 MARS EXPLORER — Interplanetary Survival Guide

### Exploring Mars. Understanding Terrain. Planning Safer Missions.

**MARS EXPLORER** is an interactive planetary exploration platform developed as part of the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, under the challenge **"Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map."**

Our goal is to develop a digital navigation and exploration system that helps future astronauts understand the Martian landscape, explore scientifically significant locations, identify potential terrain hazards, and plan safer journeys across the Red Planet.

## 🚀 Project Vision

Mars presents unique challenges for human exploration, including unpredictable terrain, steep slopes, surface obstacles, and extreme environmental conditions.

Our platform aims to bring planetary data and exploration tools together in one interactive interface, making Martian navigation more accessible, visual, and informative.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Interactive Mars Map:** Explore the Martian surface through an interactive mapping interface.
- **Location Exploration:** Discover important Martian geographical locations.
- **Terrain Visualization:** Explore planetary terrain and elevation information.
- **Hazard Identification:** Work towards identifying potentially unsafe regions.
- **Route Planning:** Develop terrain-aware navigation for safer exploration.
- **Scientific Discovery:** Connect exploration locations with NASA rover observations.
- **AI-Powered Assistance:** Future integration of an intelligent assistant for Mars exploration.

## 🛰️ Data Sources

The project aims to leverage publicly available NASA planetary datasets and scientific resources, including:

- NASA Mars Trek
- Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter (MOLA)
- NASA Mars rover mission data
